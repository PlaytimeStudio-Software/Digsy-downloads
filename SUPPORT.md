# Digsy support

Need help with Digsy, or found a problem? We're happy to help.

**Email:** support@playtime.studio

## Send us feedback from the game

The easiest way to report a problem is from inside Digsy: **Settings > Help > Feedback > Share**.
A grown-up types three numbers, then your email app opens with a message to us and Digsy's game log
already in it. Add a note about what happened and press Send. The log helps us find the problem;
it holds play times and world names, never personal information.

## Common questions

**Will I lose my worlds when I update?**
No. Worlds are saved on your device and are kept when you install a new version.

**Where are my worlds saved?**
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Playtime Studio\Digsy`
- Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/Playtime Studio/Digsy`
- iPhone, iPad and Android: inside the app. Deleting the app deletes its worlds.

**Windows says it protected my PC.** Choose **More info > Run anyway**. Digsy is new, so Windows
doesn't recognize it yet.

**My Mac won't open Digsy.** Click **Done** on the warning, then go to **System Settings > Privacy &
Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

**Android won't install the file.** Open the downloaded `.apk` on the phone and allow installing from
that source when asked. New versions install over the old one and keep your worlds.

**The controls on my phone feel wrong.** Try the other style in **Settings > Controls**: Classic
(joystick and buttons) or One Finger (tap to walk and dig, hold to place).
